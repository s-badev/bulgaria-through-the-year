4. Solar position
-----------------
The sun's position follows the NOAA Solar Calculator equations (https://gml.noaa.gov/grad/solcalc/calcdetails.html),
which are based on Jean Meeus, "Astronomical Algorithms". Sunrise and sunset are found by numerical search.
Implemented from the published equations in src/engine/sun.js.
